About WanderPath

Built by people who'd rather be on the Karakoram Highway than behind a desk.

Our Story

Started with one jeep and a Hunza itinerary

WanderPath began in 2016 when three friends kept getting asked the same question after every northern-areas trip: "who did your planning?" The answer was — nobody. We turned that into a proper operation because too many good trips were falling apart over bad logistics, not bad destinations.

Ten years on, we run fixed departures across fourteen destinations — from Hunza's orchards to Maldives villas.

🎯

Our Mission

Make it as easy to book a jeep to Deosai as it is to book a hotel room.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the first name travelers think of for both a Hunza weekend and a Maldives honeymoon.
