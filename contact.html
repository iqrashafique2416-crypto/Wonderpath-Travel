FAQ

Common questions

How far in advance should I book a tour?+

For domestic trips (Hunza, Skardu, Naran, Swat) 2–3 weeks ahead is usually enough outside peak July–August weekends. International packages and the Maldives are best booked 6–8 weeks out for the best rates and villa availability.

Are flights included in the package price?+

Domestic northern-area tours include all ground transport but not airfare to Islamabad. International packages (Dubai, Istanbul, Maldives, Malaysia, Bali, Thailand) list international airfare as excluded — we can quote it separately on request.

What's the best time to visit Hunza or Skardu?+

Late April through October, with June–August being peak season for weather and road access. Khunjerab Pass and Deosai Plains are only open roughly June to September.

Can I customize a tour itinerary?+

Yes — every package on this site is a starting template. Tell us your dates, group size and must-see stops and we'll adjust the itinerary, hotel tier or duration accordingly.

What's your cancellation policy?+

Full refund up to 14 days before departure, 50% refund between 7–14 days, and travel credit (valid 12 months) for cancellations inside 7 days, except where third-party hotels or permits carry their own non-refundable terms.
